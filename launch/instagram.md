## How every Reel works

- 10 to 25 seconds. Vertical, filmed on your phone, no intro.
- First second: the object on screen plus one line of on-screen text.
- Last on-screen line: **"Waitlist in bio."**
- Post Mon / Wed / Fri. Same file goes to TikTok and YouTube Shorts.
- Reply to every comment the same day. Comments in the first hour decide reach.

Hashtags: 3 to 5 at most, the same small set each time:
`#smartring #wearabletech #hardwarestartup #pcbdesign #stressmanagement`

## November: 12 Reels

Everything below uses things that exist today. Swap the order freely.

### Week of 2 Nov

**1. The reveal**
- Text: *I'm building a smart ring that reads stress. This is the first one that works.*
- Shots: ring in your palm, green LED on → slow turn to show the electronics inside.
- Caption: First working prototype of NERVA. Black ceramic band, flex circuit
  inside, green LED for heart rate. Most rings track your heart. This one also
  reads your skin's electrical response, the signal stress research uses.
  Waitlist in bio.

**2. What's inside**
- Text: *Everything that has to fit inside a ring.*
- Shots: flex PCB flat on the bench → finger pointing at each part → wrapped
  into the band.
- Caption: Power chip, sensors, Bluetooth, and a 22 mAh battery, on a circuit
  that bends to wrap the inside of the band.

**3. Why skin, not just heart rate**
- Text: *Your heart isn't the only thing that reacts to stress.*
- Shots: talking to camera 10 s, cut to the ring on a finger.
- Say: When you get stressed, your skin's conductance changes within a few
  seconds. Most wearables guess stress from heart rate. NERVA measures the skin
  signal directly, with two gold contacts on the inside of the band.

## Rules that keep it honest

- Renders get labelled as renders on screen. Kickstarter also bans
  photoreal renders that could pass for the finished product.
- No health claims beyond what the site says: wellness product, not a
  medical device, no "diagnose" or "prevent".
- Don't promise dates in captions until the 15 December gate is passed.
